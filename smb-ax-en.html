APPROACH / Business direction

Close the gap
between tools and work.

New tools do not change workflows on their own. We organize scattered documents and data, building AI agents and operating standards that teams can use directly.

L1 / DOCUMENTS

We connect the writing, search, summarization and review of contracts, reports and manuals to workflows, with clear human review points and responsibilities.

Applications: document generation, search, summarization and review support

L2 / KNOWLEDGE

We structure policies, manuals and meeting notes into organizational knowledge and build an AI assistant that answers within access permissions.

Applications: internal knowledge bases and source-grounded Q&A

L3 / DECISION

We deliver KPI changes, anomalies and comparisons to decision makers, supporting preparation, records and follow-up for management meetings and sales reviews.

Applications: data summaries, reporting and decision support

L4 / AUTOMATION

We redesign structured and unstructured repetitive work as agent workflows, managing execution rights, approvals, exceptions and logs together.

Applications: department agents, approvals, logs and monitoring
