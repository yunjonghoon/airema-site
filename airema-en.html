APPROACH / Business direction

Let music's story
move forward again.

AI is lowering language barriers and opening opportunities to reach global listeners. AI.ReMa respects the value and emotion of original songs and works with rights holders to create new music assets.

01 / Secure music IP rights

We review music and related assets, clarifying permitted uses and approval processes for each rights holder.

Confirm rights to recordings, works and voices, and the scope of agreements

02 / Use licensed AI

We design production workflows using authorized data and technology with confirmed licenses.

Manage data provenance, model use and approval criteria

03 / Multilingual remakes

We reinterpret lyrics, vocals and arrangements for each language and market, reviewing both musical quality and the original song's emotion.

Production, localization, quality review and rights-holder consultation

04 / New global masters / IP

We document rights, approvals and metadata for new master recordings and related production assets.

Define ownership, permitted uses and revenue sharing under the agreement

05 / Distribution and monetization

We review distribution channels such as DSPs and OTT platforms by market, connecting release planning, performance review and catalog expansion.

Global distribution strategy, performance review and catalog operation
